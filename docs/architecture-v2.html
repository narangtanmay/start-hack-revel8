Déjà-vu: the next run starts where the last one won

Scoring is three sessions on the same hidden people, and the median counts. Each new session is a clean slate for the twin, but not for us. The agent stores every thread: what it sent, what they said and did, the questions they asked, and what they asked for. The next session is told to replay the fastest API-confirmed path, compressed.

NO WIN YET · closest engaged thread instead

Keep what got replies, drop the touches that met silence or a brush-off, never repeat a fact they corrected, and offer what they asked for one touch earlier.

  1. Record. After each session, extract_thread() stores every sent touch (unsent error steps skipped), with the reply, the API events, questions ending in “?” and requests such as “please send the invite”.
  2. Rank. best_thread() picks the fastest API-confirmed win. Judge-only estimates never count as wins, and API wins are never evicted.
  3. Plan. The planner prompt gets the earlier thread, their questions to pre-answer, and a target of k−1 touches.
  4. Act. Every touch gets a hint of where the old thread stood at this point and which artifact converted.
  5. Isolate. REVEL8_MEMORY_DIR keeps tests and dry runs out of the real memory. A test fixture had been writing fake wins into it.

Persistence without self-sabotage

Rails that keep the story straight and the run valid

Real-domain block

Senders and landing pages on the target's own organisation domain are rejected before sending, because they would void the run.

Promise vs. artifact

If the mail says “PDF attached”, a PDF is attached. If we promised “no login needed”, no sign-in link follows.

Cross-turn consistency

The session ledger holds every number, date and name we stated. Drafts that contradict it are rejected and rewritten.

Channel norms

No SMS loops after silence, no WhatsApp to an email-only contact, and back to email after a call. Calls unlock when the target asks for one.

Attack the person

Prompt-injection phrasing, model refusals and simulator-style notes are blocked. A rejected draft costs a retry, not a touch.

Truth vs. estimate

Only page.submitted and attachment.opened are wins. LLM-judged disclosures are kept but labelled as estimates.
