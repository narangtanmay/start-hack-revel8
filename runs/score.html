How this is computed

Only finished practice runs with at least one touch count (38 of 49). The eval scores 3 runs: 20 × runs with an incident ÷ 3, plus 5 × best contacts ÷ your median contacts to the first incident, where a run without an incident counts as not reached. Expected averages that formula over every possible draw of 3 runs from practice (exact, closed form). Last 3 applies it to the three most recent runs. An incident is a submitted landing page or an opened attachment (API-confirmed), or a disclosure/action judged from the target's own words (an estimate; withdrawn ones never count). Practice is not scored by revel8, so this is a projection, not a result.
